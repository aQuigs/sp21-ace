# Privacy policy

Spanish 21 Trainer (also shown on the phone as Spanish 21 Ace) collects no personal data.

- The app requests no Android permissions and has no internet access, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
- Your table rules, settings, and practice and play history are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage.
- There are no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no third-party services.
- If your phone backs up app data to your own Google account (Android's standard backup), that copy is managed by Google under your account settings. The developer never receives it.
- Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes everything it stored.
